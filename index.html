OrthoLog — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 July 2026

OrthoLog is a personal surgical logbook for orthopedic surgery residents. This policy explains, in plain language, what the app does and does not do with information.

The short version: OrthoLog has no accounts, no sign-up, no company servers, no analytics, and no tracking. Everything you enter stays on your own iPhone. If you use iCloud, your logbook also syncs privately through your own Apple iCloud account, so it is backed up and follows you to a new phone — it never goes to us or to any other company. The app works fully offline, with or without iCloud.

What OrthoLog stores

Everything you type into OrthoLog — your surgical cases, the surgeons you work with, the hospitals you rotate through, your name and training year, and any private notes — is saved on your device using your iPhone's built-in on-device storage, protected by the iPhone's own encryption whenever your phone is locked. If you are signed into iCloud, this same information also syncs to your own private iCloud account (Apple's iCloud service, tied to your Apple ID) so it is backed up and follows you to a new iPhone. This uses only your personal iCloud — never our servers and never any other account — and you can turn it off simply by turning off iCloud; the app keeps working fully offline either way.

What OrthoLog does NOT do

  • It does not create an account or ask you to log in.
  • It does not send your data to us, to a company server, or to any third party. (If you use iCloud, your data syncs only to your own private iCloud account — Apple's service under your Apple ID — not to us.)
  • It does not use analytics, advertising, or any tracking of any kind.
  • It does not collect your location, contacts, photos, or any device identifiers.
  • It is designed to never store patient identifiers — no patient names, no medical record numbers, no full dates of birth, and no identifiable patient photos.

Data you choose to share

OrthoLog can create files at your request — a spreadsheet export of your cases, or a full backup of your logbook. These files are created only when you tap the button to make them, and they go only where you choose to send them (for example, saving to your Files app, or emailing them to yourself or a supervisor). OrthoLog never sends these anywhere on its own. Your private notes are excluded from the supervisor spreadsheet export; they are included only in your own personal backup file, which is meant for you.

Voice logging (optional)

OrthoLog can be opened straight to a new case using Siri, and — if you choose to set up the optional Shortcut — can pre-fill that case from what you say out loud. This works entirely through Apple's own Siri, Dictation, and Shortcuts systems, which are built into your iPhone; OrthoLog never records audio, never stores audio, and never sends anything it hears to us or to any third party. Speech you dictate is handled by Apple according to Apple's own Siri privacy practices (the same as when you dictate a text message or a note); OrthoLog only ever receives the resulting text, and only uses it to try to fill in fields on the new-case form — you always see the form before anything is saved, so nothing is logged without you reviewing it first. Setting this up is entirely optional and off by default; the app works fully without it.

Reminders

If you flag a case as an EPA (Entrustable Professional Activity) candidate, OrthoLog can remind you later to follow up on it. This uses your iPhone's own built-in local notifications — scheduled and delivered entirely on your device, with no server involved and no internet connection needed. This is on by default but can be turned off at any time in Settings.

iCloud sync (optional)

If you are signed into iCloud, OrthoLog syncs your logbook through your own personal iCloud account so it is backed up automatically and follows you if you change iPhones. This uses only Apple's iCloud service tied to your own Apple ID — never a separate account, never our servers, and only while you leave iCloud turned on. Your data in iCloud is handled by Apple under Apple's own privacy terms, the same as your other iCloud content. The app always works fully with iCloud off; syncing is a convenience, never a requirement.

Children

OrthoLog is a professional tool intended for medical residents. It is not directed at children.
