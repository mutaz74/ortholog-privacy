OrthoLog for Android — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 October 2026

This policy applies to the Android version of OrthoLog. The iPhone version has a separate policy because its features and data handling differ.

The short version: OrthoLog is a personal surgical case logbook for orthopedic residents. The Android app has no OrthoLog account, advertising, analytics, or developer-run server. It does not send your logbook or notes to the developer. Your information is stored on your device. Android's optional system backup may copy app data to your own Google account, and you can choose to save or share a backup or CSV export yourself.

Information stored on your device

OrthoLog stores the profile details you enter (such as your name, residency program, training year, rotation, and hospital), saved attending and hospital choices, your case entries, shared and private notes, and app preferences. Case entries can include dates, procedures, approaches, components, involvement levels, and EPA flags. The app uses this information to display your logbook, searches, and Insights on your phone.

OrthoLog does not ask for a patient name, medical record number, chart number, or date of birth. Free-text notes remain under your control; please do not enter patient identifiers there.

Network access and sharing

The Android app has no internet permission and does not send data to OrthoLog, the developer, advertisers, or analytics services. It has no login or synchronization service. It does not access your contacts, location, camera, microphone, or photo library.

If Android's device backup is enabled, Android may back up OrthoLog's local database and files to the Google account associated with your device and restore them to a replacement device. This is a system service under your Google account, governed by your device backup settings and Google's terms. OrthoLog does not receive that backup. App preferences and profile details are also included when you choose to create OrthoLog's manual backup file.

Files you choose to create

You can create a JSON backup containing your logbook, profile, saved choices, and private notes. You choose where Android saves that file. You can also create a CSV case export; private notes are excluded from that export. OrthoLog does not upload either file. If you save or share one through another app or cloud provider, that destination handles it under its own terms. Keep backup files private because they contain your case details and notes.

Notifications

If enabled, OrthoLog uses Android's local notifications for EPA follow-up and daily or weekly logging reminders. These reminders are scheduled on your device; no notification server is used. Notification permission can be changed in Android settings, and the reminder choices can be changed in OrthoLog Settings.

Deleting data

Settings includes a Delete all cases action. It removes cases and saved attending and hospital suggestions from this phone after confirmation, while keeping your profile and current rotation. You can remove the app and its local data through Android settings. Files you exported manually remain wherever you saved them, and copies in Android's system backup are managed through your Google account and device backup settings.

Children and medical use

OrthoLog is intended for medical residents, not children. It is a professional record-keeping tool, not a medical device, and it does not diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any medical condition. For clinical decisions, consult the appropriate qualified healthcare professional and local guidance.
